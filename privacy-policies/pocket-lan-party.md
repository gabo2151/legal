# Privacy Policy - Pocket LAN Party

**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **GGalilea** ("we", "our") handles your information when you use the mobile application **Pocket LAN Party** ("the App").

### 1. Introduction
Pocket LAN Party is a collection of mini-games you can play alone or with friends on the same local network. We do not run any servers of our own: the App has no accounts, no sign-in and no online profile.

### 2. Data Stored on Your Device
The following data is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App:
* **Settings** (difficulty, graphics quality, orientation, vibration).
* **Player name** you choose (up to 12 characters) and a **random player ID** generated by the App. The player ID is not linked to your identity, your device or any advertising identifier.

The win counter shown during a local match exists only while the match room is open and is not saved.

### 3. Local Network Play
When you host or join a match, the App connects directly to the other players' devices on the **same Wi-Fi network**, using the code you type.
* Only the data needed to play is exchanged: your player name, your random player ID and in-game information (position, drawn shapes, results).
* This data travels only inside your local network, is never sent to the internet by the App, and is not stored by other players' devices after the match.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The App uses the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:
* **Firebase Crashlytics (Google):** if the App crashes, it sends a crash report (device model, operating system version, app version, a random installation identifier and technical details of the error) so we can fix it. Crash reports do not contain your player name or the content of your games. [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **Google AdMob:** shows ads in menus and on the results screen (never during gameplay). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (e.g. in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice from the App's settings. [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google Play / Apple App Store:** process the optional "Remove ads" purchase. We do not receive or store your payment information; the App only asks the store whether the purchase is owned.

### 5. Device Permissions
* **Internet / network access:** used for local network play, ads and crash reports.
* **Local network (iOS):** used only to find and connect to other players' devices on your Wi-Fi network for multiplayer matches.

These permissions are **never** used to collect data regarding your location, contacts or files.

### 6. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.
